# EXP-003 Decision

## Decision

ACCEPT append-only contradiction and effect evidence as a core Engram evaluation invariant.

## Consequences

1. Contradictory memories remain separate historical records.
2. `CONTRADICTS`, `QUALIFIES`, and `SUPERSEDES` are explicit assessed relationships, not overwrite commands.
3. Semantic/vector similarity may nominate candidates for assessment but cannot establish contradiction by itself.
4. Harmful effect evidence is recorded as evaluation evidence; it does not silently delete or mutate the source memory.
5. Mixed beneficial/harmful evaluations remain `MIXED_EVIDENCE` rather than latest-result-wins.
6. Whether a negatively evaluated memory may be recalled or influence a future execution remains an explicit versioned policy decision.

## Next experiment class

Test competing eligible memories during one execution: multiple recalls, contradictory/qualified relationships, exact retrieval-to-decision linkage, and deterministic policy behavior when more than one operational lesson is relevant.

## Product rule

**History is preserved. Conflict is explicit. Evaluation changes interpretation, not the past.**
