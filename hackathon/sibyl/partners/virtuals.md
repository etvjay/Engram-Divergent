# Virtuals Partner Contract

Class: `PUBLISHED_BONUS`

## Published qualification
A claimed Virtuals stack counts only when judges can see a Virtuals-native integration exercised in the submitted build, such as an ACP job or a registered/transacting agent.

## Engram fit
Best use: a Virtuals agent/provider interaction generates execution experience that Engram persists through Sibyl and later uses to alter provider selection, contracting terms, verification requirements, or another autonomous decision.

## Veto
Do not use Virtuals as a decorative runtime label. The native interaction must create or consume decision-relevant experience.

## Required evidence
- protocol-native ACP/agent/job evidence;
- exercised in demo;
- trace linking interaction -> persisted memory -> fresh-session changed behavior.

## Negative test
Remove the Virtuals interaction. If the flagship scenario and product meaning remain unchanged, do not claim the partner stack.

Status: `UNVERIFIED / OPTIONAL`.
